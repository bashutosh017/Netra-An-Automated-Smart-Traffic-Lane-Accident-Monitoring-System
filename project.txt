
Netra: Automated Smart Traffic Lane & Accident Monitoring System

• The Real-World Problem: Traffic enforcement cameras capture millions of hours of footage, but identifying traffic law violations, wrong-way drivers, or accidents still requires human supervisors to review video logs manually.
• How it Works (Similar to Jaagran AI): An AI platform where traffic operators upload a surveillance video feed or stream a live CCTV camera to auto-detect gridlock, wrong-way driving, or vehicle lane violations.
• The AI/ML Twist: Combine Canny Edge Detection and Hough Line Transformations to dynamically map lane boundaries on a road. Then, overlay a YOLO model to track vehicles crossing those virtual boundary lines.
• The Production Architecture:
	• Frontend: A clean dashboard displaying the filtered video feed alongside dynamic counters for total vehicles, speed estimations, and traffic alerts.
	• Backend: A processing server built on Flask or FastAPI utilizing OpenCV to decode video inputs frame-by-frame.
	• Database: A PostgreSQL table recording automated traffic alerts: vehicle_type, timestamp, infraction_severity (Warning, Critical)
