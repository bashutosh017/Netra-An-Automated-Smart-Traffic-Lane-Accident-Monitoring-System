# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want Netra to be a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free project for everyone, regardless of age, background, experience level, gender, identity, ability, ethnicity, nationality, religion, or appearance.

We pledge to treat each other with kindness and to focus on what is best for the project and its community.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and patient, especially with beginners
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully
- Assume good intent, and ask for clarification before assuming the worst
- Credit other people's work and ideas
- Keep discussion on topic and useful
- Respect privacy, including the privacy of people who appear in traffic footage

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats, or personal attacks
- Discriminatory jokes or language
- Sexualized language or imagery
- Trolling or deliberately derailing discussions
- Publishing someone's private information, such as their address or personal contact details, without permission
- Posting real footage that identifies people (faces, license plates) without a legitimate need and proper permission
- Any other conduct that would be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Responsibilities of Maintainers

Maintainers will clarify these standards and respond fairly when problems arise. They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently restrict anyone whose behavior is harmful.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces, including the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions, and any channel used to represent the project in public.

## Reporting

If you experience or see unacceptable behavior, please report it privately to:

## Enforcement

Maintainers may respond to violations in these ways, depending on how serious and how repeated they are:

1. **Reminder:** a private note explaining what was inappropriate
2. **Warning:** a formal warning about continued behavior
3. **Temporary restriction:** a time-limited ban from project spaces
4. **Permanent ban:** removal from all project spaces for serious or repeated violations
